# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Account & Profile

### How do I create an account?
Download the app, tap Sign Up, enter your email and create a password. Verify your email to activate your account.

### Can I change my username?
Yes, go to Profile → Edit Profile → Username. You can change it anytime.

### How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

### What is the Privacy Matrix?
Privacy Matrix lets you control who can see your profile, posts, streams, and other content. Choose from Public, Friends Only, or Private presets, or customize each setting individually.

## KYC Verification

### Why do I need KYC verification?
KYC (Know Your Customer) verification is required to:
- Withdraw money from wallet
- Sell items on marketplace
- Receive tips from live streaming
- Access premium features

### How long does KYC verification take?
Most verifications are completed within 24-48 hours. You'll receive a notification when reviewed.

### What documents are accepted?
- Government-issued ID (passport, driver's license, national ID)
- Clear photos with all text readable
- Valid (not expired) documents

### My KYC was rejected. What should I do?
Check the rejection reason in your profile. Common issues:
- Blurry or unclear photos
- Expired documents
- Name mismatch with account
- Document not fully visible

Re-submit with corrected documents.

### Can I use someone else's ID?
No. Using another person's ID violates our terms and will result in permanent ban.

## Wallet & Payments

### How do I add money to my wallet?
Go to Wallet → + Add Credits → Choose amount → Pay with card. Credits are added instantly.

### What payment methods are accepted?
We accept major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express).

### How do I send a tip to someone?
In their profile or chat → Send Money → Enter username → Choose amount → Add optional message → Send.

### What is the minimum withdrawal amount?
Minimum withdrawal is $10.

### How long do withdrawals take?
- Bank Transfer (free): 3-5 business days
- Instant Payout (1.5% fee): Within 1 hour

### Are there any fees?
- Adding credits: No fee
- Sending tips: No fee
- Bank withdrawals: Free
- Instant withdrawals: 1.5% fee
- Marketplace transactions: 5% platform fee for sellers

### What if a payment fails?
Check your card details and try again. Contact support if the issue persists.

## Marketplace

### How does escrow protection work?
When a buyer pays, money is held securely by us. It's only released to the seller after:
- Buyer receives item
- Buyer submits delivery proof
- Buyer confirms item is as described
- 48-hour dispute window passes

### What if the item doesn't arrive?
You have 48 hours after expected delivery to open a dispute. We'll review evidence and may issue a full refund.

### What is delivery proof?
Photos you take when receiving a package showing:
- Unopened package
- Tracking label
- Delivery timestamp
This protects both buyers and sellers.

### Can I cancel an order?
- Before shipping: Contact seller to request cancellation
- After shipping: Only through dispute process if item is not as described

### How do I list an item for sale?
Marketplace → + New Listing → Add photos → Description → Price → Condition → Publish

### What makes a good listing?
- Multiple clear photos from different angles
- Honest description of condition
- Accurate measurements or specifications
- Fair pricing based on condition
- Detailed shipping and return policy

## CAP (Capture Evidence)

### What is CAP?
Capture Evidence (CAP) is our verified photo/video feature that records with dual cameras and embeds metadata like GPS location, timestamp, and device information for authenticity.

### Why use CAP instead of regular camera?
CAP provides verifiable proof with:
- Dual camera recording (front + back)
- Automatic GPS tagging
- Tamper-proof timestamp
- Device metadata
- Evidence-grade quality

Perfect for documentation, disputes, or journalism.

### Can I edit CAP photos?
No. CAP evidence cannot be edited to maintain integrity. You can retake before uploading.

### What is dual camera mode?
Records from front and back cameras simultaneously, showing both what you're capturing and the person capturing it. Available in:
- Picture-in-Picture (PIP)
- Split Screen

### Does CAP work offline?
CAP requires:
- GPS signal
- Internet connection
- IMU sensors
- Dual camera capability

All must show green tick before capturing.

## Live Streaming

### How do I start a live stream?
Tap + → Go Live → Add title and description → Choose category → Start Stream

### Can I monetize my streams?
Yes, viewers can send tips during your stream. You need KYC verification to withdraw earnings.

### What are stream rules?
- Keep content appropriate for general audience
- No nudity or sexual content
- No harassment or hate speech
- Respect all viewers
- Follow all community guidelines

### Can I save my streams?
Public streams are automatically saved to your profile. Private streams are not saved.

### How do I end a stream?
Tap End Stream → Confirm. You'll see viewer statistics and earnings.

## Security & Privacy

### How do I enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?
Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication → Turn On → Follow setup instructions

### What is biometric security?
Add Face ID or Touch ID to secure your account. Go to Settings → Security → Manage Biometrics

### How can I see active sessions?
Settings → Security → Active Sessions shows all devices logged into your account. You can logout from any device remotely.

### Someone else is using my account. What should I do?
1. Change your password immediately
2. Enable 2FA
3. Go to Active Sessions and logout all other devices
4. Contact support if unauthorized activity occurred

## Support

### How do I contact support?
- In-app: Settings → Help & Support
- Email: support@app.com
- Response time: Usually within 24 hours

### How do I report a user or content?
Tap the three dots menu on any post, profile, or stream → Report → Choose reason → Submit

### What happens when I report something?
Our moderation team reviews all reports within 24 hours. Action is taken based on severity and policy violations.

### Can I appeal a moderation decision?
Yes, you can appeal within 30 days through Settings → Support → Appeal Decision

## Troubleshooting

### App is crashing
1. Update to latest version
2. Restart your device
3. Clear app cache
4. Reinstall app if issue persists

### Videos not playing
- Check internet connection
- Try lowering video quality in settings
- Clear app cache

### Can't upload photos/videos
- Check storage space
- Verify permissions are granted
- Check file size (max 100MB per file)

### Notifications not working
Settings → Notifications → Enable all relevant categories and ensure device settings allow notifications.
